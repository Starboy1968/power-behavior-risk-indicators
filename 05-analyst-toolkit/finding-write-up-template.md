# Qualitative Finding Template

**Title:** describe the condition, not the person (for example "Risk acceptances approved without independent review," not "SVP ignores security")

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Condition** | What you found. Counts, dates, document references, quoted *record* text. |
| **Evidence and grade** | Sources used (observation / interview / documents) · Grade: single observation, repeated pattern, or corroborated pattern · Limits of the review |
| **Criteria** | Internal policy first; then framework reference (for example COSO 2013 Principle 3; NIST CSF 2.0 GV.RR-02) |
| **Cause** | Process or structural reason (workflow gap, unclear authority, incentive design, missing forum practice) |
| **Effect** | What risk is created or hidden, and for whom |
| **Recommendation** | Specific, proportionate, owned, dated |
| **Management response** | Recorded verbatim, with owner and date |

## Quality check before issuing
- [ ] Every statement can be traced to a source
- [ ] At least two independent sources for anything reported as a finding
- [ ] No characterization of personality, motive, or intent
- [ ] No unconsented attribution of interview comments
- [ ] Nonverbal observations are not used as evidence
- [ ] Management has reviewed for factual accuracy
- [ ] Legitimate explanations (authorized management intervention, real emergencies) considered and addressed

## Wording guide
| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "The lead bullied the team into approving." | "In 3 of 4 sampled approvals, the request stated 'leadership already decided' and approval followed within the same day." |
| "The committee is a rubber stamp." | "Across three observed meetings, most items were approved without questions recorded." |
| "They're hiding the real status." | "Reported status was green for five cycles while the defect tracker showed open critical items in the same period." |
| "Nobody dares speak up." | "Several interviewees independently said they would not raise concerns in the forum; two cited a prior concern that was not followed up." |
